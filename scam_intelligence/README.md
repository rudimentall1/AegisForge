# AegisForge Scam Intelligence

Defensive crypto-forensics layer for detecting, tracing and documenting
scam infrastructure.

## Pipeline

seed address / transaction
        ↓
ScamHunter
        ↓
Transaction Tracer
        ↓
Entity Linker
        ↓
Evidence Engine
        ↓
Attribution Scoring
        ↓
Human Review
        ↓
Intelligence Report

## Principles

- Facts and inferences are always separated.
- Attribution requires evidence.
- No private credentials or unauthorized access.
- Public attribution requires human review.
- Confidence scores are evidence-based.
- Every conclusion must be traceable to supporting observations.
